1. Overview & Developer Commitment

Welcome to Assist Gaming & Tech Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and confidentiality of our users across our mobile applications distributed on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Our core development philosophy is Privacy by Design: we prioritize on-device data processing and minimized data collection. We never sell, monetize, broker, or exploit personal or financial user data.

🤖 BillSnap AI — Dedicated Privacy Disclosures Official Disclosures

BillSnap AI (Bundle Identifier: com.billsnap.ai) is an intelligent receipt scanner, expense tracker, and IRS Form 1040 Schedule C tax organization assistant. Due to the financial nature of receipt and tax records, the following strict privacy principles apply:

A. Camera and Photo Library Access

  • Camera Permission: Used solely when you actively take a photo of a physical receipt, invoice, or tax slip. Real-time camera feeds are never recorded or streamed.
  • Photos / Media Permission: Used solely to allow you to import existing receipt images or PDF invoices from your device storage.

B. AI Optical Character Recognition (OCR) & Google Gemini Processing

🔒 Zero-Training Guarantee: Receipt images sent for AI text extraction are processed in transient volatile memory. Neither Assist Studio nor Google uses your personal receipts, tax deductions, or financial transactions to train public artificial intelligence models.

  • Receipt images are securely transmitted to Google Gemini Vision API endpoints using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS 256-bit encryption.
  • Processing is strictly stateless: once receipt fields (vendor, date, total, tax, currency, and line items) are parsed, the image payload is discarded from memory.

C. 100% On-Device Local Storage (SQLite)

  • All your parsed receipts, category assignments, merchant notes, and IRS Schedule C tax profiles are stored exclusively on your device in an isolated, sandboxed SQLite database.
  • Assist Studio does not operate a remote cloud database holding your receipts or tax records. If you are offline, BillSnap AI continues to function locally.

D. Cloud Backup Options (Apple iCloud Drive & Google Drive)

  • Cloud backups are 100% optional and user-directed.
  • iOS (iCloud Drive): Backup files are saved directly into your personal iCloud Drive container or shared via the native iOS Files app.
  • Android (Google Drive): Backup files are saved into your private Google Drive app folder via authenticated Google OAuth.
  • We do not have access to your iCloud or Google Drive credentials, encryption keys, or backup files.

E. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

  • In-app purchases and PRO subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple App Store (StoreKit) on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android.
  • Assist Studio never sees, processes, or retains your credit card details, bank account numbers, or billing addresses.

F. IRS Form 1040 Schedule C & Financial Disclaimer

  • BillSnap AI organizes expenses according to standard IRS Schedule C expense categories (Lines 8 through 27b) and provides tax reference checklists.
  • BillSnap AI is an automated productivity tool designed for self-preparation. It does not provide certified legal, certified public accounting (CPA), or financial advice (IRS Circular 230). Users remain responsible for verifying their final tax filings.

G. Complete Right to Data Deletion

  • You maintain total ownership of your data. You can delete individual receipts or purge your entire database at any time via Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally stored data.
