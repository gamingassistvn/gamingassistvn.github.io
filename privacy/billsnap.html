Privacy Policy for BillSnap AI

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 07, 2026

1. Introduction & Core Principle

Assist Gaming & Tech Studio ("we", "our", or "us") operates BillSnap AI ("the Application"). We believe that your personal and financial data belongs exclusively to you. The Application is built on a Privacy-by-Design principle: All financial data is processed locally on your device, and we do not sell, rent, or monetize your receipts or personal financial information.

2. Data Collection and Processing

BillSnap AI collects only the minimal data strictly necessary to provide receipt scanning, expense organization, and tax deduction calculation:

  • Camera & Photos: When you choose to scan a receipt or select a receipt image from your gallery, the image is used solely to extract text, merchant, date, and amount.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Receipt analysis utilizes Gemini Vision AI models. Images transmitted for OCR are processed securely and ephemeral; they are not used to train public AI models.
  • Financial Records: Saved expense records, tax classifications (IRS Schedule C lines), notes, and mileage logs are stored locally in the app's encrypted local database on your physical device.

3. Cloud Backup (User-Controlled)

The Application provides optional cloud backup through Apple iCloud and Google Drive. These backups are stored within your own private cloud account under your personal credentials. Assist Studio has no access to your cloud storage files or credentials.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application may integrate trusted third-party services:

  • Apple In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing: To manage Pro subscriptions safely through official app store payment gateways.
  • Crash Diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports to maintain stability and performance.

5. Data Deletion & Retention

Because all records reside on your device, deleting an entry or clearing application data permanently removes the records immediately. You can delete all your data at any time via App Settings → Data Management → Clear All Data.
