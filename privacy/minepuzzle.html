Privacy Policy for MinePuzzle Daily

Effective Date: October 2026 | Last Updated: October 07, 2026

1. Overview

MinePuzzle Daily is a 3D puzzle challenge game developed by Assist Gaming & Tech Studio. We are dedicated to transparent, safe, and family-friendly privacy practices.

2. Information Collection

  • Game Progress & High Scores: Stored locally on your device. When participating in global leaderboards, anonymous game IDs and completion times are synchronized securely.
  • Device Information: Basic device hardware parameters (screen resolution, OS version) to ensure stable 60–120 FPS graphics rendering.

3. Advertising & Analytics

MinePuzzle Daily uses verified ad networks (Google AdMob) to support free gameplay:

  • Ads adhere strictly to Google Play Families Policy and Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT).
  • Users can opt out of personalized tracking at any time via system settings or consent prompts.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
