Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Who is BillSnap AI designed for?
BillSnap AI is crafted specifically for self-employed individuals, independent 1099 contractors, rideshare and delivery drivers (Uber, Lyft, DoorDash), freelancers, salon/nail shop owners, and small business operators who file IRS Form 1040 Schedule C.
❓ How is my receipt data protected?
We operate under a strict Privacy by Design standard. All receipt records and tax computations remain isolated on your local phone storage in an encrypted SQLite database. When communicating with the Gemini Vision API, images are transmitted over secure TLS/HTTPS and never stored or used to train public AI models.
❓ Can I export my data to Excel for my CPA?
Yes! BillSnap AI generates multi-sheet Excel workbooks (.xlsx) with embedded formula calculations, summary KPI scorecards, and formatted itemized breakdowns ready to hand directly to your CPA, tax preparer, or TurboTax / H&R Block.
❓ Does BillSnap AI replace my accountant?
No. BillSnap AI is an automated self-help productivity organizer. Under IRS Circular 230, it is not a substitute for certified legal or CPA advice. It helps you keep spotless, organized records so you never miss a deduction or panic during tax season.
