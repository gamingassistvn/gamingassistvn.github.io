Quick 3-Minute Setup Guide

Step-by-step visual instructions matching the English & Vietnamese app interface

STEP 1 • FLOATING BAR

Enable "Appear on Top" Permission

Allows T11 Auto Helper to display the compact Start/Stop control bar on screen.

STEP 2 • GESTURE ENGINE

Enable Accessibility Touch Service

Turn on Auto Click Service inside Android Settings → Accessibility → Installed Apps.

STEP 3 • GREEN ROUTINE

Calibrate Green Condition Points

Drag the numbered target marker onto your screen's Green icon, Free button, and Close button.

STEP 4 • RED ROUTINE

Calibrate Red Morale Points & Run

Save the Red Morale positions once, then tap GREEN, RED, or BOTH anytime you need.

🛡️ Accessibility Service & Privacy Disclosure

T11 Auto Helper is an independent Android productivity & gesture utility developed by Assist Gaming & Tech Studio. It uses the official Android AccessibilityService API solely to perform user-configured screen taps and countdown timers initiated explicitly by the user. It does not collect personal data, does not access accounts or files of other apps, and can be turned off at any time in Android Settings. Requires Android 11.0 or newer.
